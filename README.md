Gobble Dash is a 2D canvas turkey escape game: outrun the farmer across the farmyard, the barnyard and the kitchen, jump the fences, slide under the washing lines, time the dropping carving knife, leap the hot oven, hide in the haystacks and grab the corn — and reach the Freedom Gate, 1500 m away, before Thanksgiving dinner catches you. Keyboard and mouse, with a HUD of score, distance, hearts and the farmer's gap, synthesized sound, and end screens for caught and escaped.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```
